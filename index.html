When to Call Your Doctor or Visit Labor & Delivery

ACOG / NHS
  • Urgent Severe or persistent abdominal/pelvic pain or cramping.
  • Immediate Heavy vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage (potential water break).
  • Call Promptly Significantly decreased or absent baby movements (fetal movement slowdown).
  • Urgent Sudden swelling of face/hands, severe persistent headaches, or visual disturbances (spots, flashing lights).
  • Monitor Closely Fever over 100.4°F (38°C) or severe persistent vomiting/inability to keep liquids down.
